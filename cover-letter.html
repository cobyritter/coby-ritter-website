Dear Hiring Committee,

I am writing to express my interest in pursuing opportunities in investment banking. As a Finance and Real Estate student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, I have developed a strong interest in the way financial decisions shape businesses, markets, and communities. I am drawn to investment banking because it combines strategic thinking, rigorous analysis, and the ability to create meaningful impact at a large scale.

My academic training and extracurricular involvement have helped me build a foundation in financial analysis, market research, and strategic problem-solving. Through my work in finance and real estate coursework, along with participation in clubs focused on investment banking and capital markets, I have grown increasingly interested in the fast-paced, intellectually demanding environment of banking. I enjoy learning how businesses evaluate opportunities, prepare for transactions, and make decisions that can influence long-term growth.

What excites me most about investment banking is the opportunity to work with smart, driven people in a highly collaborative environment where learning is constant and execution matters. I value the challenge of turning complex information into clear recommendations, and I am motivated by the chance to contribute to major decisions that affect both business strategy and economic progress.

I believe my curiosity, work ethic, and interest in financial strategy make me a strong fit for this path. I am eager to continue developing my skills, contribute meaningfully, and pursue a career where I can learn quickly, add value, and help shape important outcomes.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background and goals align with your firm or team.

Sincerely,
Coby Ritter
